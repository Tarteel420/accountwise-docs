The Admin Dashboard provides a **consolidated financial view of the entire
organization**, so Admin and Manager users can monitor overall performance at a
glance.

<Callout type="info">
  Accessible only to **Admin** and **Manager** roles. Data reflects complete,
  system-wide transactions with no user-level filtering.
</Callout>

<Screenshot src="/images/dashboards/admin-dashboard.png" alt="Admin Dashboard overview" caption="The Admin Dashboard — revenue, expenses, outstanding invoices/bills, profit and loss, invoice status, and cash flow trend at a glance." />

## What it shows

By default, the dashboard shows these widgets:

| Widget | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Total Revenue | Total revenue, with the percentage change. |
| Total Expenses | Total expenses, with the percentage change. |
| Outstanding Invoices | Total amount of outstanding customer invoices. |
| Outstanding Bills | Total amount of outstanding vendor bills. |
| Profit & Loss Summary | Income vs expenses by month. |
| Invoice Status | Breakdown of invoices by Paid, Pending, and Overdue. |
| Cash Flow Trend | Cash movement over time. |
| Top Customers | Highest-revenue customers, with their number of invoices. |
| Expenses Breakdown | Expenses by category. |
| Bills Breakdown | Breakdown of bills by Paid, Pending, and Overdue. |

You can add more widgets or hide these from **Edit Dashboard → Customize**. See
[Customizing Your Dashboard](/docs/dashboards/customizing-your-dashboard).

The data covers all customers, vendors, sales, and purchases, giving one unified
snapshot of the business. It updates dynamically as transactions are recorded.

## Reporting period

The dashboard includes a reporting period filter (default **Current Year**). See
[Dashboards](/docs/dashboards) for the full list of period options.
