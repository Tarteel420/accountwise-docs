title: Salesperson Dashboard

---

The Salesperson Dashboard gives a **focused view of sales-related performance**
for the logged-in user's assigned customers.

<Callout type="info">
  Accessible only to the **Accountant (Salesperson)** role. Data is filtered to
  the user's assigned customers and related sales documents only — no
  company-wide data.
</Callout>

## What it shows

- Total Invoices, Total Bills
- Pending Sale Orders, Approved Sale Orders
- Outstanding Invoices, Outstanding Bills, Bills Breakdown
- Pending Purchase Orders, Completed Purchase Orders
- Total Credit Memos, Total Refunds
- Top Customers, Invoice Status

All calculations reflect the assigned-customer mapping and update dynamically as
transactions are recorded.

## Reporting period

Includes the same reporting period filter as other dashboards (default **Current
Year**). See [Dashboards](/docs/dashboards).
