Welcome to **AccountWise** — the financial management system that records and
manages all of your business's financial transactions, from customer sales to
vendor purchases, while keeping your accounting correct in the background.

This documentation is written for everyone who uses AccountWise: admins setting
the system up, accountants and salespeople managing day-to-day transactions, and
managers reviewing reports.

## New here? Start with onboarding

If this is your first time, follow the onboarding path. It explains the core
ideas first, then walks you through your first real transactions in order.
