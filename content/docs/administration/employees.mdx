The **Employees Management** section manages employee records within
AccountWise. Employees are used in [Expense
Reports](/docs/purchases/expense-reports).

## The Employees list

Go to **Employees Management → Employees**. The list shows each employee's
**Name**, **Job Title**, **Supervisor**, **Department**, **Location**, and
**Email**, with actions to **view, edit, clone, and delete**.

<Screenshot src="/images/administration/employee-toolbar.jpg" alt="Employees page toolbar" caption="(1) Export, (2) Import, (3) New Employee, (4) Supervisor filter, (5) Search." />

- Use **Supervisor** to filter employees by supervisor, or **Search** to find an
  employee.
- Use **Export** to export the list, or **Import** to bulk import employees.

## Creating an employee

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
Go to **Employees Management → Employees** and select **New Employee**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter organizational details
Enter the **Employee Name** (required), and optionally the **Job Title**,
**Supervisor**, **Department**, **Location**, and **Notes**.

<Screenshot src="/images/administration/employee-new-annotated.jpg" alt="New Employee form" caption="(1) Employee Name, (2) Job Title, (3) Supervisor, (4) Department, (5) Location, (6) Notes, (7) Address." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add the address
Under **Address**, enter **Address Line 1**, **Address Line 2**, **City**,
**State / Province**, **Country**, and **Zip Code** if needed.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add contact information
Under **Contacts → Primary Contact**, enter the **First Name**, **Email**, and
**Phone Number** (required), and optionally **Last Name**, **Mobile Number**,
and **Title**. Select **Add Secondary Contact** to add another contact.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Optionally add **Attachments**, then select **Create**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Viewing an employee

Select **View** (eye) on a row to open the employee's page. The page shows the
employee's **Department**, **Supervisor**, and **Job Title**, followed by the
**Details**, **Contacts**, **Addresses**, **History**, **Attachments**, and
**Transactions** tabs.

### Transactions

The **Transactions** tab lists all of the employee's transactions in one place:

- **Expense Reports** — date, expense report number, due date, paid amount,
  status, and amount.
- **Bill Payments** — date, payment number, payment method, status, and amount.

Select a document number to open it. When there are more records than shown,
select **View all expense reports** to open the full list.
