---
title: Closing Date
description: Lock the books up to a chosen date.
---

The **Closing Date** setting defines the date up to which your books are
closed.

<Callout type="warn">
  Once a closing date is saved, new documents **cannot** be created for dates
  before the closing date.
</Callout>

## Setting the closing date

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Closing Date
Go to **Settings → Closing Date**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose the date
Under **Choose the Closing Date**, pick the **Closing Date**. The current
setting is shown below the field as **Previous Closing Date**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Select **Save Closing Date**.

<Screenshot src="/images/administration/closing-date.jpg" alt="Closing Date settings" caption="(1) Closing Date, (2) Previous Closing Date, (3) Save Closing Date." />
</Step>

</Steps>
