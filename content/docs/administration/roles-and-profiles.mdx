User Management has three sections: **Users**, **Roles**, and **Profiles**.
Role-based access ensures users only see and act where relevant to their
responsibilities.

## Roles & Permissions

Roles and permissions are managed from inside AccountWise — you don't need to
open the separate identity (IAM) application. Go to **Settings → Role &
Permission**. The page lists the roles your organization can assign in
AccountWise.

<Callout type="info">
  **Role & Permission** is only shown to users with access to role
  management.
</Callout>

<Screenshot src="/images/administration/roles-list.jpg" alt="Roles & Permissions page" caption="(1) New Role, (2) Managed by Account Wise badge, (3) Duplicate, (4) View people and View permissions, (5) Custom badge, (6) row menu, (7) Edit permissions." />

Each role card shows the role's name, how many people hold it, its category
(for example, **Administration**, **Operational**, or **Read-only**), and its
description. There are two kinds of roles:

| Kind | Badge | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Built-in roles** | **Managed by Account Wise** | **View people**, **View permissions**, and **Duplicate**. Their permissions are read-only. |
| **Custom roles** | **Custom** | **View people** and **Edit permissions**. The row menu (**⋮**) has **Duplicate** and **Delete**. |

<Screenshot src="/images/administration/roles-custom-menu.jpg" alt="Custom role menu" caption="(1) Duplicate or Delete a custom role." width={560} />

### Built-in roles

| Role | Description shown in the app |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Platform administrator — full access to every module. |
| **Accountant** | Full access to every module. |
| **Manager** | Read-only. Can view the ledger, dashboard, audit trail, and reports — no create, edit, or full access anywhere. |

Your organization may also have other built-in or custom roles — for example,
an auditor role.

### Viewing the people in a role

Select **View people** to see who holds the role, with each person's **Name**,
**Email**, and **Locations** (for example, **Whole organization**).

<Screenshot src="/images/administration/roles-people.jpg" alt="People with a role" caption="The people who hold the role. Email addresses are blurred in this example." width={560} />

### Creating a role

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Create a role
Select **New Role**. The **Create a role** dialog opens. The new role exists in
your organization only — the roles managed by AccountWise stay read-only.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose how to start

- **Start blank** — nothing is granted. Build the role up feature by feature.
  The role grants nothing until you set permissions, so nobody gains access just
  because it exists.
- **Duplicate a role** — copy an existing role's permissions, then adjust them.
  Pick the role under **Copy permissions from**; each role shows how many
  grants it has across how many modules, and how many people hold it.

<Screenshot src="/images/administration/roles-create.jpg" alt="Create a role — Start blank" caption="(1) Start from, (2) Role name, (3) Category, (4) category suggestions, (5) Description, (6) Create and set permissions." width={600} />

<Screenshot src="/images/administration/roles-duplicate.jpg" alt="Create a role — Duplicate a role" caption="(1) Duplicate a role, (2) the role to copy permissions from." width={600} />
</Step>

<Step>
### Name the role
Enter a **Role name** (required) — it is shown on the role card and beside
everyone who holds it. Optionally enter a **Category** (or pick a suggestion
such as **Operational**, **Managerial**, **Executive**, or **Compliance**) and a
**Description**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Continue to permissions
Select **Create and set permissions** to create the role and go on to set its
permissions. See [Editing permissions](#editing-permissions).
</Step>

</Steps>

### Editing permissions

Select **Edit permissions** on a custom role. The page shows the role's
**Role name**, **Category**, and **Description** (which you can change), and
**View people**.

<Screenshot src="/images/administration/roles-edit-permissions.jpg" alt="Edit permissions page" caption="(1) Role name, Category, and Description, (2) Search modules and features, (3) Apply to everything, (4) module with its granted count and Set access, (5) access level buttons, (6) Save changes." />

Permissions are grouped by module, and each module shows how many permissions
are granted (for example, **33/33**):

| Module | Features |
| --- | --- |
| **Accounting** | Accounts, Journal, Ledger, Reconciliation, Reconciliation rules, Closing, Budget, Fixed assets, Bank transfers, Audit trail |
| **Organization** | Organization, Locations, Items, Saved searches, Dashboard, Customer field map, User Management, Customer Lifecycle Transitions, Approvals |
| **Purchasing (A/P)** | Bills, Purchase orders, Purchase returns, Vendor credits, Expenses, Expense reports, Expense reversals, Vendor payments, Credit card payments, Additional surcharges |
| **Vendor management** | Vendors, Employees |
| **Sales (A/R)** | Invoices, Sale orders, Credit memos, Receipts, Refund receipts, Refund settlements, Bank deposits |
| **Customer management** | Customers, Customer assignment |
| **Shared CRM sub-resources** | Activities, Notes — a grant here applies to both vendors and customers |
| **Reporting** | Reports |

For each feature, choose an access level: **View**, **Create**, **Edit**, or
**Full**. Some features also have an extra permission — **Void** for Bills,
Expense reports, and Invoices, and **Bypass** for Approvals. A dash (**–**)
means the level isn't available for that feature — for example, the Audit
trail, Dashboard, and Reports are read-only by design.

To change many permissions at once:

- Use **Set access** on a module to set every feature in that module.
- Use **Apply to everything** to set every feature in every module: **Remove all
  access**, **View only**, **Create & edit access**, or **Full access**.
- Use **Search modules and features** to find a feature.

<Screenshot src="/images/administration/roles-apply-everything.jpg" alt="Apply to everything options" caption="(1) Apply to everything options." />

Your changes are listed at the bottom of the page (*No changes yet* until you
change something). Select **Save changes** to save them, or **Cancel**.

### Viewing a built-in role's permissions

Select **View permissions** on a built-in role to see its permissions. The page
is read-only — granted levels are highlighted, and the others are greyed out.

<Screenshot src="/images/administration/roles-view-managed.jpg" alt="Read-only permissions of a built-in role" caption="(1) Read-only and Managed by Account Wise, (2) View people, (3) levels not granted, (4) a granted level." />

### Assigning roles to users

When creating a [user](/docs/administration/users), the admin assigns a role.
Every available role is also listed wherever roles are selected — for example,
in [Customer Lifecycle
Transitions](/docs/administration/customer-lifecycle-transitions).

## How permissions shape the application

Access in AccountWise is checked **per module**, and the application adapts to
the permissions of the signed-in user:

- **Sidebar items** only appear when the user has **view** access to that
  module. For example, **Invoices** appears under **Sales** only for users who
  can view invoices, and a menu group such as **Sales** or **Purchase** only
  appears when the user can view at least one item in it.
- **Customization** pages under **Settings → Customization** only appear for
  users with **full** access to the related document. See
  [Customization](/docs/administration/customization).
- While a page loads, AccountWise briefly shows **Checking your access…** as it
  confirms the user may open that page.

## Record-level access

Some settings go further than module access and decide **which records** a role
can act on. In [Customer Lifecycle
Transitions](/docs/administration/customer-lifecycle-transitions), each role is
given an access level:

- **All Records** — can act on any Lead or Customer record.
- **Own Records** — can act only on Leads and Customers assigned to them. See
  [Customer Assignments](/docs/sales/customers/customer-assignments).

## Approvals

Approvers and approval policies are set up per document in **Settings →
Approval Policy**, and approvers review requests from **Approvals** in the
sidebar. Both pages are only shown to users with access to approvals. See
[Approval Workflow](/docs/administration/approvals).
