The **Locations** settings let you manage the locations your business operates
from. Locations are used on documents, as a filter across many
[reports](/docs/reports), and in [budgeting](/docs/banking/budgeting).

## The Locations list

Go to **Settings → Locations**. The list shows each location's **Name**,
**City**, **State**, **Zip Code**, and **Country**, with actions to edit or
delete it. Use **Search** to find a location.

## Adding a location

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
On the Locations page, select **New Location**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the location details
Enter the **Name** and choose the **Branch** (both required). Optionally add a
**Description**, the **Address** and **Address 2**, **City**, **Zip Code**,
**Country** (defaults to **United States**), **State**, and **Phone Number**.

Enter a **Document Prefix** to add a prefix to the numbers of documents
created for this location — for example, bank transfers for a location with
the prefix **82** are numbered **82-BT-0001**.

</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Select **Create**, or **Cancel** to discard it.
</Step>

</Steps>
