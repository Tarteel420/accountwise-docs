title: Expenses
description: Record direct purchases that do not need a formal bill.
---

An **Expense** records a direct purchase or payment that does not require a
formal [Bill](/docs/purchases/bills) — typically day-to-day operational costs
paid immediately.

## The Expense list

Go to **Purchase → Payments → Expenses**. The list shows the **Vendor Name**
(with the expense number), **Payment Date**, and **Amount**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/expense-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Expense list" caption="(1) New Expense, (2) Vendors filter, (3) Search, (4) View and See more." />

## Creating an expense

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
On the Expense page, select **New Expense**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the vendor and payment details
Choose the vendor in **Select Vendor** — the **Mailing Address** is filled in.
Enter a **Reference number** (optional) and **Email**, check the **Tax Type**,
**Discount Type**, and **Payment Date**, and choose the **Payment Account** and
**Payment Method** (for example, **Credit Card**). Optionally choose a
**Location**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/expense-new-annotated.jpg" alt="New Expense form" caption="(1) Select Vendor, (2) Mailing Address, (3) Reference number, (4) Email, (5) Tax Type, (6) Discount Type, (7) Payment Date, (8) Payment Account, (9) Payment Method, (10) Location." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add items
Under **Details**, use **Search AccountWise Items** or **Add Item** to add the
items or services purchased, and enter any **tax** and **discount**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Add any **Attachments**, complete the **Terms and Conditions** (required), and
select **Create** or **Create & Send**. The system records the financial impact
in a single step.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Correcting an expense

If an expense was entered incorrectly, use an [Expense
Reversal](/docs/purchases/expense-reversal).
