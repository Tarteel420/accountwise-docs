---
title: Voiding Purchase Documents
description: Void bills and purchase orders, reversing their journal entries.
---

Voiding cancels a purchase document and **reverses all of its related journal
entries**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Void is **configuration based** — not everyone has the option to void.
</Callout>

## What can be voided

- **Bills** — only **unpaid** bills can be voided.
- **Purchase Orders.**

## Void a bill

Go to **Purchase → Bills**. On the bill's row, open the **See more** menu
(**⋮**) and select **Void**. Enter the reason and confirm.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/bill-row-actions.jpg" alt="Bill See more menu with Void" caption="The See more menu (2) on a bill row includes Void." width={720} />

Voided bills appear under the **Void** tab of the Bills list.

## Void reason

When voiding, you must add a **void reason**. It is included in the email sent
to the vendor.
