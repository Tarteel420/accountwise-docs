A **Credit Card Payment** records a purchase made using a credit card.

## The Credit Card Payments list

Go to **Purchase → Payments → Credit Card Payments**. The list shows the
**Credit Card Account Name** (with the payment number), **Bank Account Name**,
**Vendor Name**, **Payment Date**, and **Amount**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/credit-card-payment-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Credit Card Payments list" caption="(1) New Credit Card Payment, (2) Credit Cards filter, (3) Search." />

## Creating a credit card payment

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
On the Credit Card Payments page, select **New Credit Card Payment**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Complete the payment
Complete the fields:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Credit Card Account | Required. The current **Account Balance** is shown below it. |
| Vendor (Optional) | The vendor, if any. |
| How much did you pay? | The amount. Required. |
| Date of Payment | Defaults to today. |
| Payment Account | The account the funds come from. Required. |
| Description (Optional) | Optional notes. |
| Location | Optional. |

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/credit-card-payment-new-annotated.jpg" alt="New Credit Card Payment form" caption="(1) Credit Card Account, (2) Account Balance, (3) Vendor, (4) How much did you pay?, (5) Date of Payment, (6) Payment Account, (7) Description, (8) Location." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Optionally add **Attachments**, then select **Create**.
</Step>

</Steps>

The system tracks the expense while recording the **liability** against the
credit card account for later settlement.

<Callout type="info">
  Credit card activity is summarized in the [Credit Card Payments
  Report](/docs/reports/purchase-reports).
</Callout>
