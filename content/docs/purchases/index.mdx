title: Purchases

---

The Purchases module covers the money-out side of the business: your **vendors**
and the **purchase documents** used to record what you buy and pay for.

Vendors are managed from **Vendors Management** in the sidebar. Purchase
documents are under **Purchase**: **Bills**, **Payments** (**Bill Payments**,
**Expenses**, and **Credit Card Payments**), **Purchase Orders**, **Vendor
Credits**, **Purchase Return**, **Expense Reversal**, and **Expense Report**.

## Common behaviors

Across all purchase documents:

- Listing pages can be **filtered by vendor**, and most can also be filtered
  by date and searched.
- A **Paid Amount** column is shown on the Bills and Expense Report lists.
- Every document shows **History** and **GL Impact** at the bottom.
