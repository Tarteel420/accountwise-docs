title: Purchase Orders

A **Purchase Order (PO)** requests goods or services from a vendor. It formalizes
procurement before a bill is generated and gives visibility into expected
payables.

## The Purchase Orders list

Go to **Purchase → Purchase Orders**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/purchase-order-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Purchase Orders list" caption="(1) New Purchase Order, (2) status tabs, (3) Vendors, Start date, End date, and Search filters, (4) View, Edit, Delete, and See more." />

- **Status tabs** — **All**, **Draft**, **Pending**, **Complete**,
  **Cancelled**, **Void**, and **Linked**, each with a count.
- **Columns** — Vendor (with the PO number), Bill, Purchase Order Date, Payment
  Term, Quantity, Amount, Status, and Actions.
- **See more** menu — **Clone**, **Cancel**, and **Mark as Linked**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/purchase-order-row-actions.jpg" alt="Purchase order See more menu and bill link" caption="(1) The See more menu: Clone, Cancel, Mark as Linked. (2) The bill created from a PO, shown as a link." />

## Creating a purchase order

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
Go to **Purchase → Purchase Orders** and select **New Purchase Order**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the vendor and enter the header details
Choose the vendor in **Select Vendor** — the mailing address populates
automatically. Enter a **Reference number** (optional) and **Email**, and check
the **Payment Term**, **Due Date**, **Tax Type**, **Discount Type**, and
**Purchase Order Date**. Optionally choose a **Location**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/purchase-order-new-annotated.jpg" alt="New Purchase Order form" caption="(1) Select Vendor, (2) Mailing Address, (3) Reference number, (4) Email, (5) Payment Term, (6) Due Date, (7) Tax Type, (8) Discount Type, (9) Purchase Order Date, (10) Location." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add items
Under **Details**, search for items and use **Add Item** to add lines with
quantities and pricing.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Add any **Attachments** and **Terms and Conditions**, then select **Save as
Draft**, **Create**, or **Create & Send**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Purchased For field

Purchase orders can include a **Purchased For** dropdown: *SJ Computers*,
*Start Used Computers*, or *Both*. This field is customizable and appears only
when it is enabled for your organization.

## Converting to a bill

When a PO is converted to a [Bill](/docs/purchases/bills), the bill number
appears in the **Bill** column of the PO list as a **hyperlink** that opens that
bill.

<Callout type="info">
  Purchase orders support the [approval workflow](/docs/administration/approvals). When
  your organization turns on **Requires Approval** for this document in
  **Settings → Approval Policy**, a new or changed document must be approved
  before it takes effect.
</Callout>
