A **Vendor Credit** is created when:

- Goods are returned to a vendor
- An overpayment is made
- A vendor issues an adjustment

## The Vendor Credit list

Go to **Purchase → Vendor Credits**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/vendor-credit-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Vendor Credit list" caption="(1) New Vendor Credit, (2) All, Draft, Created, Partial, and Applied tabs, (3) Vendors, Start date, End date, and Search filters." />

The list shows the Vendor (with the credit number), Vendor Credit Date, Amount,
Applied Amount, and Status, with **View**, **Edit**, **Delete**, and **See
more** actions on each row.

## Creating a vendor credit

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
Go to **Purchase → Vendor Credits** and select **New Vendor Credit**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the vendor and enter the header details
Choose the vendor in **Select Vendor** — the **Mailing Address** is filled in.
Enter the **Reference Number** and **Email**, check the **Vendor Credit Date**,
and optionally link a **Purchase Order Number** and choose a **Location**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/vendor-credit-new-annotated.jpg" alt="New Vendor Credit form" caption="(1) Select Vendor, (2) Reference Number, (3) Email, (4) Purchase Order Number, (5) Vendor Credit Date, (6) Location." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add details and account
Under **Details**, enter a **Description**, select the appropriate **Account**,
and enter the **Quantity** and **Rate** — the **Amount** is calculated. Select
**Add Item** for more lines.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Add any **Attachments**, complete the **Terms and Conditions** (required), and
select **Save as Draft**, **Create**, or **Create & Send**. The vendor credit is
then available to apply later.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Applying vendor credits

Vendor credits reduce the outstanding liability and can be applied later during
[Bill Payment](/docs/purchases/bill-payments) (tick **Use Vendor Credit**) or
[Bill](/docs/purchases/bills) creation.

<Callout type="info">
  Vendor credits also affect vendor balances in reports. See [Vendor
  Reports](/docs/reports/vendor-reports).
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Vendor credits support the [approval workflow](/docs/administration/approvals). When
  your organization turns on **Requires Approval** for this document in
  **Settings → Approval Policy**, a new or changed document must be approved
  before it takes effect.
</Callout>
