title: Bill Payments

A **Bill Payment** records money paid to a vendor — or to an employee, for
expense-report payments.

## The Bill Payments list

Go to **Purchase → Payments → Bill Payments**. The list shows the **Name** (with
the payment number), **Bill Payment Date**, and **Amount**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/bill-payment-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Bill Payments list" caption="(1) New Bill Payment, (2) Vendors filter, (3) Employee filter, (4) Search, (5) View and See more." />

## Recording a payment

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
On the Bill Payments page, select **New Bill Payment**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose who is being paid
Choose **Vendor** or **Employee** (for expense-report payments), then select the
vendor or employee. The outstanding balance appears on the right.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the payment details
Choose the payment **Account**, and check the **Payment Date** and **Payment
Method**. Optionally select a **Location**. Tick **Use Vendor Credit** to apply
an existing vendor credit.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/bill-payment-new-annotated.jpg" alt="New Bill Payment form" caption="(1) Vendor, (2) Vendor or Employee, (3) outstanding balance, (4) Account, (5) Payment Date, (6) Payment Method, (7) Location, (8) Use Vendor Credit, (9) Find Bill No." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Apply the payment to bills
Under **Outstanding Bills**, the open bills are listed with their **Due Date**,
**Original Amount**, and **Open Balance**. Tick the bills being paid and enter
the **Payment** for each — full or partial payment is supported. Use **Find
Bill No.** to find a specific bill.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Optionally add **Attachments**, then select **Create** or **Create & Send**.
</Step>

</Steps>

If the payment is partial, the bill remains **partially open** and Accounts
Payable updates accordingly.
