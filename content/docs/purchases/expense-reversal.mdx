---
title: Expense Reversal
description: Reverse an incorrect or unwanted expense entry.
---

An **Expense Reversal** handles incorrect or unwanted expense entries. It is the
exact opposite of the [Expense](/docs/purchases/expenses) document and contains
similar fields and structure, so the experience stays consistent.

## The Expense Reversal list

Go to **Purchase → Expense Reversal**. The list shows the Vendor Name (with the
reversal number), Expense Reversal Date, and Amount.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/expense-reversal-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Expense Reversal list" caption="(1) New Expense Reversal, (2) Vendors filter, (3) Search." />

## Creating an expense reversal

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
Go to **Purchase → Expense Reversal** and select **New Expense Reversal**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the vendor and payment details
Choose the vendor in **Select Vendor**, enter a **Reference number** (optional)
and **Email**, check the **Reversal Date**, and choose the **Payment Account**
and **Payment Method**. Optionally choose a **Location**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/expense-reversal-new-annotated.jpg" alt="New Expense Reversal form" caption="(1) Select Vendor, (2) Reference number, (3) Email, (4) Reversal Date, (5) Payment Account, (6) Payment Method, (7) Location." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add items to reverse
Under **Details**, use **Search AccountWise Items** or **Add Item** to add the
same items from the original expense being reversed.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Add any **Attachments**, complete the **Terms and Conditions** (required), and
select **Create** or **Create & Send**. The system records the reverse
accounting impact automatically.
</Step>

</Steps>

## What it does

When created, the system records the **reverse accounting impact** of the
original expense transaction.
