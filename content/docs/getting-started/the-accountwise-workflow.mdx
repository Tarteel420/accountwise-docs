---
title: The AccountWise Workflow
description: How sales and purchases flow through the system from start to finish.
---

AccountWise organizes business activity into two mirrored cycles: **sales**
(money coming in) and **purchases** (money going out). This page shows the big
picture so the individual guides make sense.

## The sales cycle

The sales cycle begins with a customer and ends with payment received.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Create the customer
Every sale starts from a customer record. See [Creating
Customers](/docs/sales/customers/creating-customers).
</Step>

<Step>
### Create a sales order or invoice
Depending on your process, begin with a [Sales
Order](/docs/sales/sales-orders) or go straight to an
[Invoice](/docs/sales/invoices).
</Step>

<Step>
### Recognize revenue
When the invoice is created, AccountWise records the journal entry, recognizes
revenue, and creates Accounts Receivable automatically.
</Step>

<Step>
### Record the payment
When the customer pays, record an [Invoice
Receipt](/docs/sales/invoice-receipts). Accounts Receivable is reduced.
</Step>

</Steps>

## The purchase cycle

The purchase cycle mirrors sales, but starts with a vendor and ends with
payment made.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Create the vendor
See [Creating Vendors](/docs/purchases/vendors/creating-vendors).
</Step>

<Step>
### Create a purchase order or bill
Begin with a [Purchase Order](/docs/purchases/purchase-orders) or go straight
to a [Bill](/docs/purchases/bills).
</Step>

<Step>
### Record the liability
When the bill is created, AccountWise records the journal entry and creates
Accounts Payable automatically.
</Step>

<Step>
### Pay the vendor
Record a [Bill Payment](/docs/purchases/bill-payments). Accounts Payable is
reduced.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  You do not post journal entries yourself — AccountWise does it as you create
  these documents. See [Journal Entries](/docs/accounting/journal-entries).
</Callout>
