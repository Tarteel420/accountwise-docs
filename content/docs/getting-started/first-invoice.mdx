---
title: Create Your First Invoice
description: A short walkthrough for creating an invoice and recognizing revenue.
---

This quickstart creates a direct invoice — the fastest way to bill a customer.

<Callout type="info">
  Prerequisite: at least one [customer](/docs/sales/customers/creating-customers)
  and one [item](/docs/administration/items).
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Invoices
Go to **Sales → Invoices** and select **New invoice**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/invoice-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Invoices list" caption="(1) New invoice." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the customer
In **Select Customer**, choose the customer. Their balance, billing and
shipping addresses, email, and payment term are filled in automatically.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/invoice-new-1.jpg" alt="New invoice — customer and header fields" caption="(1) Select Customer. The other fields fill in from the customer record." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add items
Use **Search AccountWise Items** or **Add Item** to add the products or services
being sold, with the quantity and rate. Rate accepts up to **4 decimal places**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/invoice-new-3.jpg" alt="Invoice item line and totals" caption="(19) An item line, (22) the totals update as you go." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Select **Save as Draft**, or **Create & Send** to create the invoice and email
it to the customer. AccountWise records the journal entry, recognizes revenue,
and creates Accounts Receivable.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/invoice-new-4.jpg" alt="Invoice save buttons" caption="(25) Save as Draft, (26) Create & Send." />
</Step>

</Steps>

## What happens next

The invoice status stays **Unpaid** until payment is recorded. When the customer
pays, record the payment — see [Record a
Payment](/docs/getting-started/recording-a-payment).
