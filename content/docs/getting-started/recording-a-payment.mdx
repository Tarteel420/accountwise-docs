---
title: Record a Payment
description: How to record money received from a customer or paid to a vendor.
---

Payments close the loop on the sales and purchase cycles. This quickstart covers
both directions.

## Money received (customer)

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the invoice
Go to **Sales → Invoices** and select **View** on the invoice.
</Step>

<Step>
### Mark as paid
Select **Mark as Paid**. In **Mark Invoice as Paid**, enter the **Amount** (full
or partial), **Payment Method**, **Payment Account**, and **Payment Date**,
optionally attach files, and select **Mark as Paid**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/invoice-mark-paid.jpg" alt="Mark Invoice as Paid dialog" caption="(2) Amount, (3) Payment Method, (4) Payment Account, (5) Payment Date, (6) Mark as Paid." />
</Step>

</Steps>

To apply one payment to several invoices, create an [Invoice
Receipt](/docs/sales/invoice-receipts) from **Sales → Receipts → Invoice
Receipts** instead.

If the payment is partial, the invoice status becomes **Partial** and Accounts
Receivable updates accordingly.

## Money paid (vendor)

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Bill Payments
Go to **Purchase → Payments → Bill Payments** and select **New Bill Payment**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose the vendor and payment details
Select the vendor, then choose the payment **Account**, and check the **Payment
Date** and **Payment Method**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/bill-payment-new-annotated.jpg" alt="New Bill Payment form" caption="(1) Vendor, (4) Account, (5) Payment Date, (6) Payment Method." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Apply the payment and save
Under **Outstanding Bills**, tick the bills being paid and enter the **Payment**
for each (full or partial). Select **Create**, or **Create & Send**.
</Step>

</Steps>

If the payment is partial, the bill remains partially open and Accounts Payable
updates. See [Bill Payments](/docs/purchases/bill-payments).
