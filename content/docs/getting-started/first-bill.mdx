---
title: Create Your First Bill
description: A short walkthrough for recording a vendor bill and its liability.
---

This quickstart records a direct bill — the fastest way to capture what you owe
a vendor.

<Callout type="info">
  Prerequisite: at least one [vendor](/docs/purchases/vendors/creating-vendors)
  and one [item](/docs/administration/items).
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Bills
Go to **Purchase → Bills** and select **New Bill**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/bill-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Bills list" caption="(1) New Bill." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the vendor
In **Select Vendor**, choose the vendor. The **Mailing Address** is filled in
from the vendor record. Check the **Payment Term**, **Bill Date**, and **Due
Date**.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/bill-new-1.jpg" alt="New Bill form" caption="(1) Select Vendor, (5) Payment Term, (6) Bill Date, (7) Due Date, (11) Search AccountWise Items." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add items and amounts
Use **Search AccountWise Items** or **Add Item** to add the items received. The
amount field is editable — if you change the amount, the rate updates
automatically to match.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Select **Create** to create the bill without emailing the vendor, **Create &
Send** to email it, or **Save as Draft**. AccountWise records the journal entry:
the expense or asset account is debited and Accounts Payable is created.

<Screenshot src="/images/purchases/bill-new-2.jpg" alt="Bill save options" caption="(12) Save as Draft, (13) Create, (14) Create & Send." />
</Step>

</Steps>

## What happens next

The bill stays **open** until fully paid. When you pay, record a [Bill
Payment](/docs/purchases/bill-payments).
