---
title: What is AccountWise?
description: A role-based financial management system for recording and managing business transactions.
---

AccountWise is a **financial management system** designed to record and manage
all of a business's financial transactions. It handles the full accounting
cycle — from customer sales to vendor purchases — and automatically maintains
the correct journal entries in the background.

## What you can do with AccountWise

- Manage customers and run the full sales cycle (orders, invoices, payments).
- Manage vendors and run the full purchase cycle (orders, bills, payments).
- Keep an accurate Chart of Accounts and journal entries automatically.
- Handle banking: transfers, deposits, and reconciliation.
- Plan with budgets and track fixed assets.
- Analyze the business with a large library of financial reports.

## Accounting happens automatically

You do not need to be an accountant to use AccountWise. When you create a normal
document — such as an invoice or a bill — the system records the correct debit
and credit entries for you in the background.

<Callout type="info">
  Every financial transaction ultimately posts to the [Chart of
  Accounts](/docs/accounting/chart-of-accounts). Understanding that idea makes
  the rest of the product much easier to follow.
</Callout>

## Built around roles

AccountWise is **role-based**. What each person sees and can do depends on their
assigned role. This keeps access controlled and appropriate to each person's
responsibility.
