AccountWise controls access through **roles**. Every user is assigned one role,
which determines the modules and actions available to them.

## The built-in roles

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Platform administrator — full access to every module. |
| **Accountant** | Full access to every module. |
| **Manager** | Read-only — can view the ledger, dashboard, audit trail, and reports. |

These descriptions are shown on each role's card in **Settings → Role &
Permission**, where you can also view each role's exact permissions and create
custom roles. See [Roles &
Profiles](/docs/administration/roles-and-profiles#roles--permissions).

<Callout type="info">
  The sidebar only shows the modules your role can view. Your organization may
  also have roles beyond these. See [Roles &
  Profiles](/docs/administration/roles-and-profiles).
</Callout>

## Assigned customers

When a customer is assigned to a salesperson, that salesperson can only see
their **own** assigned customers and the related documents. This keeps
visibility controlled and accountability clear. Customers can be assigned to
one or more salespersons from [Customer
Assignments](/docs/sales/customers/customer-assignments).
