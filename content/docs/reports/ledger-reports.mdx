These reports let you trace and verify the underlying accounting. Open them from
**Reports → General Ledger** and **Reports → Trial Balance**. For the filter
bar, see [Running a report](/docs/reports#running-a-report).

## General Ledger

Lists all general ledger entries and transactions, so you can track the detailed
history of each account.

| Filter | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | The account or accounts to show. |
| **Report Period** / dates | The period to cover. |
| **Location** | Limit the report to a location. |
| **Search** | Find specific entries. |
| **Customer**, **Vendor**, **Employee** | Limit the report to entries for a customer, vendor, or employee. |
| **Transaction Type** | Limit the report to a type of transaction. |

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/general-ledger-filters.jpg" alt="General Ledger filters" caption="(1) Account, (2) Advanced, (3) Location, Search, Customer, Vendor, Employee, and Transaction Type, (4) Customize Columns." />

The report shows **Date**, **Account**, **Description**, **Type**, **Customer**,
**Vendor**, **Employee**, **Debit**, **Credit**, and **Balance** by default.

### Choosing columns

Select **Customize Columns** (gear icon) to choose the columns. Tick a column to
show it or untick it to hide it, and use the arrows to change the order. Columns
marked **(Required)** can't be hidden. Optional columns include **Location** and
document numbers such as **Invoice Number**, **Bill Number**, and **Journal Entry
Number**. Select **Save Changes**, or **Reset to Default** to restore the
original columns.

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/customize-columns.jpg" alt="Customize Columns dialog" caption="(1) Required columns can't be hidden, (2) move a column up or down, (3) show or hide a column, (4) Reset to Default, (5) Save Changes." width={640} />

<Callout type="info">
  To check a bank account before or after a [Bank
  Reconciliation](/docs/banking/bank-reconciliation), filter the General Ledger
  on that account.
</Callout>

## Trial Balance

Shows the balance of every account, so you can confirm that total debits equal
total credits.

- **Filters** — Report Period (or dates) and **Location**.
- **Columns** — **Name**, **Debit**, and **Credit**.
