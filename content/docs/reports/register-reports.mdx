---
title: Register Reports
description: The Asset Register and Bank Register.
---

Open these reports from **Reports → Asset Register** and **Reports → Bank
Register**. For the filter bar, see [Running a
report](/docs/reports#running-a-report).

## Asset Register

Detailed information about all [fixed assets](/docs/banking/fixed-assets) in
the system.

- **Filters** — Report Period (or dates), **Location**, and **Category**.
- **Columns** — Asset Number, Name, Category, Location, Purchase Date, Purchase
  Price, Accumulated Depreciation, Depreciation Method, Net Book Value, and
  Status.

## Bank Register

Details of the bank accounts and their transactions: the list of bank accounts,
the **Opening Balance** of each, and all of their transactions.

- **Filters** — Report Period (or dates), **Accounts**, and **Locations**.
- **Columns** — **Account Name** and **Opening Balance**, followed by the
  account's transactions.
