---
title: Salesperson Reports
description: Sales performance by receipt, order, sales rep, and invoice.
---

These reports analyze sales performance from different angles. Open them from
**Reports → Salesperson Reports**. For the filter bar, see [Running a
report](/docs/reports#running-a-report).

| Report | Purpose | Report filters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sales by Sales Receipt** | Revenue recorded through sales receipts. | Salesperson |
| **Sales by Sales Order** | Order values and sales trends based on orders placed. | Salesperson |
| **Sales by Sales Rep** | Sales performance of each sales rep. | Salesperson |
| **Sales by Invoice** | Revenue generated through invoices. | Salesperson, Status |
