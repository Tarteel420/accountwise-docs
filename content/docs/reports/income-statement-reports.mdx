---
title: Income Statement Reports
description: How the business performs over time — summary, detail, comparison, monthly, cash flow, and by location.
---

These reports show how the business is performing over time. Open them from
**Reports → Income Statement**. For the filter bar, see [Running a
report](/docs/reports#running-a-report).

| Report | Purpose | Report filters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Summary** | Income and expenses by account, grouped into sections such as **Revenue**, with totals. | Location |
| **Detail** | Detailed income and expense breakdown; shows **Net Income** at the end. | Locations |
| **Comparison** | Compare periods — YTD, year-over-year, or previous period — and the variance between them. | Comparison Type, Location |
| **Profit & Loss by Months** | Month-by-month P&L, with a column for each month and a **Total**. | Location |
| **Cash Flow** | How cash moves through the business, based on income statement data. | Location |
| **Location Based** | The income statement separated by location, with a column for each location and a **Total**, showing each location's contribution. | Locations |
