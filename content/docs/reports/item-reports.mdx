---
title: Item Reports
description: Sales and purchases by item, in summary and detail.
---

These reports analyze activity by item. Open them from **Reports → Item
Reports**, then choose **Sales by Item** or **Purchase by Item** and
**Summary** or **Detail**. For the filter bar, see [Running a
report](/docs/reports#running-a-report).

| Report | Purpose | Report filters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sales by Item – Summary** | Which items generate the most revenue: quantity sold and value per item. | Customer Name, Item, Location |
| **Sales by Item – Detail** | The sales transactions behind each item. | Customer Name, Item, Location |
| **Purchase by Item – Summary** | How much of each item was purchased, and its value. | Vendor Name, Item, Location |
| **Purchase by Item – Detail** | The purchase transactions behind each item. | Vendor Name, Item, Location |

The summary reports show **Item Name**, **Description**, **Quantity Sold** (or
**Quantity Purchased**), **Unit Price**, and **Total Revenue** (or **Total
Expense**).
