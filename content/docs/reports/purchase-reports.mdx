---
title: Purchase Reports
description: Purchase orders, weekly purchases, and credit card payments.
---

These reports analyze purchasing activity. Open them from **Reports → Purchase
Reports**. For the filter bar, see [Running a
report](/docs/reports#running-a-report).

## Purchase by Purchase Order

Purchasing trends and totals by purchase order.

- **Filters** — **Vendor Name** and **Status**.
- **Columns** — Vendor, Date, PO Number, Payment Term, Quantity, Converted to
  Bill, Status, and Amount.

## Weekly Purchase Report

Purchases for the period, based on bills by default. Use the **Type** filter to
change the documents included, for example to purchase orders.

- **Columns** — Date, Doc Type, Doc No., Vendor, Item Type, Item Details, SERI
  Category, Quantity, Amount, and Payment Status.

## Credit Card Payments Report

A consolidated view of transactions paid by credit card — bill payments
processed by credit card and expenses where the payment method is a credit
card.

- **Filters** — **Account**.
- **Columns** — Date, Doc Type, Doc No., Vendor, Reference, Account, and Amount.
