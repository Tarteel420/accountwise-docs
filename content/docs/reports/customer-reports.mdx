---
title: Customer Reports
description: Receivables, customer balances, and sales by customer.
---

These reports focus on money owed by customers and customer sales activity.
Open them from **Reports → Customer Reports**. For the filter bar, see [Running
a report](/docs/reports#running-a-report).

| Report | Purpose | Report filters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AR Aging Summary** | Outstanding receivables per customer, grouped into aging buckets. | Customer Name, Days, Period, Location |
| **AR Aging Detail** | Open balance for each customer invoice with its aging status. | Customer Name, Days, Period, Location |
| **Customer Balance Summary** | Total outstanding **Balance** per customer. | Customer Name |
| **Customer Balance Detail** | Each customer's transactions with amount, open balance, and running balance. | Customer Name |
| **Customer By Sales Rep Report** | Customers grouped by their assigned sales rep. | Salesperson |
| **Sales by Customer** | Total sales generated by each customer. | Location |
| **Invoice List** | All invoices with amount and open balance. | Location |
| **Open Invoices** | Open invoices across customers. | Customer Name |
| **Sales Raw Data** | Open invoices plus open credit memos, with full document details. | Customer Name, Type |

All of these reports also use the **Report Period** or the **Start Date** and
**End Date**, and most have a **Location** filter under **Advanced**.

## Aging buckets

In the AR aging reports, **Days** sets the length of each aging bucket (30 by
default) and **Period** sets how many buckets there are (4 by default) — giving
**Current**, **1-30**, **31-60**, **61-90**, and **Over 90**. **Location** is
under **Advanced**.

## Report columns

| Report | Columns |
| --- | --- |
| AR Aging Summary / Detail | Customer, Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, Over 90, Total |
| Customer Balance Summary | Customer Name, Balance |
| Customer Balance Detail | Date, Transaction Type, Num, Due Date, Amount, Open Balance, Balance |
| Open Invoices | Date, Transaction type, Num, Terms, Due date, Open balance |
| Sales Raw Data | Customer, Type, Doc #, Date, Status, Location, Sales Person, Due Date, Term, Age, Due Age, Balance |

<Callout type="info">
  Customer balance reports include [Credit
  Memos](/docs/sales/credit-memos): balances reflect **Outstanding Invoices −
  Credit Memos**, so the closing balance matches the ledger closing balance.
  This applies to AR Aging Summary, AR Aging Detail, Customer Balance Summary,
  and Customer Balance Detail.
</Callout>
