---
title: Vendor Reports
description: Payables, vendor balances, and open and paid bills.
---

These reports focus on money owed to vendors. Open them from **Reports → Vendor
Reports**. For the filter bar, see [Running a
report](/docs/reports#running-a-report).

| Report | Purpose | Report filters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AP Aging Summary** | Outstanding payables per vendor, grouped into aging buckets. | Vendor Name, Days, Period |
| **AP Aging Detail** | Individual bills with their aging status, to identify overdue payments. | Vendor Name, Days, Period |
| **Vendor Balance Summary** | Total balance payable per vendor. | Vendor Name |
| **Vendor Balance Detail** | Each vendor's transactions and how the balance changes. | Vendor Name |
| **Open Bills** | All unpaid vendor bills. | Vendor Name |
| **Paid Bills** | Bills already paid, with total, paid, and remaining amounts. | Vendor Name |

All of these reports also use the **Report Period** or the **Start Date** and
**End Date**, and most have a **Location** filter under **Advanced**. In the AP
aging reports, **Days** sets the length of each aging bucket and **Period** sets
how many buckets there are, as in the [AR aging
reports](/docs/reports/customer-reports#aging-buckets).

## Report columns

| Report | Columns |
| --- | --- |
| AP Aging Summary / Detail | Vendor, Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, Over 90, Total |
| Vendor Balance Summary | Vendor Name, Balance |
| Vendor Balance Detail | Date, Transaction Type, Num, Due Date, Amount, Open Balance, Balance |
| Paid Bills | Date, Type, Num, Due date, Total Amount, Paid Amount, Remaining |

<Callout type="info">
  Vendor balance reports include [Vendor
  Credits](/docs/purchases/vendor-credits): balances reflect **Outstanding Bills
  − Applied/Available Vendor Credits**, so the closing balance matches the ledger
  closing balance. This applies to AP Aging Summary, AP Aging Detail, Vendor
  Balance Summary, and Vendor Balance Detail.
</Callout>
