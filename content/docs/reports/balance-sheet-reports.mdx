---
title: Balance Sheet Reports
description: The financial position of the business — summary, detail, comparison, cash flow, and by location.
---

These reports show the financial position of the business. Open them from
**Reports → Balance Sheet**. For the filter bar, see [Running a
report](/docs/reports#running-a-report).

| Report | Purpose | Report filters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Summary** | Assets, liabilities, and equity by account, with totals. | Location |
| **Detail** | Detailed view of assets, liabilities, and equity for all balance sheet accounts. | Locations |
| **Comparison** | Compare across periods — YTD and previous period — with variance analysis. | Comparison Type, Location |
| **Cash Flow** | Cash position over time: balances of cash and bank accounts and how they change. | Location |
| **Location Based** | The balance sheet separated by location, with a column for each location and a **Total**. | Locations |
