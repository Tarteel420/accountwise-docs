**Bank Reconciliation** matches your bank statement with the transactions
recorded in AccountWise, so that the adjusted balance in your books agrees with
the statement ending balance.

## Reconciliation History

Go to **Banking → Bank Reconciliation**. The **Reconciliation History** page
lists every reconciliation.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/reconciliation-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Reconciliation History" caption="(1) New Reconciliation, (2) All, Draft, and Completed tabs, (3) Filter by Account, (4) Search, (5) row actions." />

- **Tabs** — **All**, **Draft**, and **Completed**, each with a count.
- **Columns** — Account Name (with the reconciliation number and account type),
  Reconciliation Date, Statement Ending Date, Statement Ending Balance, Ledger
  Balance, Status, and Actions.
- **Actions** — **View** (eye), **Edit** (pencil), **Export**, and **Delete**
  (bin). A completed reconciliation can't be edited or deleted; the tooltips
  read **Cannot edit completed reconciliation** and **Cannot delete completed
  reconciliation**.

## Starting a reconciliation

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
On the Reconciliation History page, select **New Reconciliation**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose the account
In **Which account do you want to reconcile?**, select the **Account**.
AccountWise then shows the date of the last statement for that account (for
example, **Last statement ending 06/18/2026**) and how many uncleared
transactions are available for reconciliation.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/reconciliation-new-1.jpg" alt="Choose the account to reconcile" caption="(1) Account, (2) last statement ending date, (3) uncleared transactions available." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Upload the bank statement (optional)
Under **Upload Bank Statement (Optional)**, drag and drop your bank statement
CSV file or select **Browse Files**. Uploaded statement lines are matched
automatically with your ledger entries. Only CSV files are supported, up to
10 MB. Select **Download Template** to get the CSV layout to use.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the statement balances
The **Beginning Balance** is filled in from the previous reconciliation of the
account. Enter the **Statement Ending Balance** from your bank statement and
check the **Statement Ending Date** (it defaults to today).

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/reconciliation-new-2.jpg" alt="Upload bank statement and statement balances" caption="(4) Upload Bank Statement CSV, (5) Download Template, (6) Beginning Balance, (7) Statement Ending Balance, (8) Statement Ending Date." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Start reconciling
Select **Start Manual Reconciling**. The button is available once the required
fields are complete.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Reconciling transactions

The reconciliation page shows the account and ending date, a balance summary,
and instructions.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/reconciliation-workspace-1.jpg" alt="Reconciliation page" caption="(1) Account and Ending Date, (2) Ledger Balance, Statement Ending Balance, Adjusted Balance, Unpresented Transactions, Uncleared Transactions, and Difference, (3) Instructions." />

<Steps>

<Step>
### Tick the reconciled transactions
Under **Transactions to Reconcile**, tick **Reconciled** for each transaction
that appears on your bank statement. Use the **All**, **Payments**, and
**Deposits** tabs to filter the list. The table shows the **Date**, **Cleared
Date**, **Description**, **Type**, **Debit**, and **Credit**.

Transactions that were matched automatically from the uploaded statement have a
green left border and a **Matched** badge. You can tick or untick any
transaction, whether or not it was matched automatically.
</Step>

<Step>
### Check the difference
As you tick transactions, the summary updates:

- **Unpresented transactions** are credit transactions (payments you've made)
  recorded in your books but not yet cleared by the bank.
- **Uncleared transactions** are debit transactions (money received) recorded
  in your books but not yet processed by the bank.
- **Adjusted Balance** = Ledger Balance + Unpresented Transactions − Uncleared
  Transactions.

The goal is a **Difference** of **$0.00** between the statement ending balance
and the adjusted balance.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
In the **Reconciliation Summary**, select **Save Reconciliation** to complete
the reconciliation, or **Save as Draft** to finish it later. Select **Back to
Setup** to return to the previous step.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/reconciliation-workspace-3.jpg" alt="Reconciliation Summary and save buttons" caption="(1) Reconciliation Summary, (2) Back to Setup, (3) Save as Draft, (4) Save Reconciliation." />
</Step>

</Steps>

To continue a draft, open the **Draft** tab and select **Edit** on its row.

## Viewing a completed reconciliation

Select **View** on a row to open the reconciliation.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/reconciliation-view.jpg" alt="Completed reconciliation" caption="(1) Account Name, dates, Reconciliation Status, Reconciliation Number, and Description, (2) Export, (3) Reconciliation Summary." />

The page shows:

- **Reconciliation Summary** — Statement Ending Balance, Balance as per Books,
  Balance as per Bank Statement, Adjusted Balance as per Books, Uncleared
  Transactions Sum, and Unpresented Transactions Sum.
- **Reconciled Transactions** — each transaction with its **Match Status**
  (**Manual** or **Matched**).
- **Unpresented Transactions** and **Uncleared Transactions** left over at the
  time of reconciliation.
- **History** of the reconciliation.

Select **Export** to download the reconciliation.

<Callout type="info">
  You can also review an account's transactions in the [General
  Ledger](/docs/reports/ledger-reports) by filtering on the bank account.
</Callout>
