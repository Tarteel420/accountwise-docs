title: Fixed Assets

---

**Fixed Assets** registers the assets your organization owns, calculates their
depreciation automatically, and records their disposal.

## The Fixed Assets list

Go to **Fixed Assets Management → Fixed Assets** in the sidebar. The same menu
also contains [Fixed Assets Categories](/docs/banking/fixed-asset-categories).

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/assets-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Fixed Assets list" caption="(1) New Asset, (2) status tabs, (3) Location filter, (4) Search, (5) View." />

- **Status tabs** — **All**, **Created**, **Depreciating**, **Depreciated**, and
  **Disposed**, each with a count.
- **Filters** — **Location** and **Search**.
- **Columns** — Asset Code, Asset Name, Status, Purchase Date, Cost, Method, Net
  Book Value, and Actions.

Select **View** (eye) to open an asset.

## Creating an asset

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
On the Fixed Assets page, select **New Asset**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Link the purchase entry
In **Debit Entry**, search for and select the debit transaction that recorded
the asset purchase. Each entry shows the account, location, date, and amount.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the asset information
Enter the **Asset Name**, and optionally choose a **Category** and
**Location**. Enter the **Purchase Cost**, and check the **Purchase Date** and
**Depreciation Start Date** (both default to today). Optionally add a
**Description**.

When you choose a **Category**, the depreciation details — method, useful life,
depreciation account, and accumulated depreciation account — are filled in from
the [category](/docs/banking/fixed-asset-categories). A message under the field
confirms this, for example *Depreciation details filled in from Vehicles
Pakistan. You can change them.* Any change you make applies to this asset only.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/assets-new-category-prefill.jpg" alt="Category selected on a new asset" caption="(1) Choosing a Category fills in the depreciation details." />

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/assets-new-1.jpg" alt="New Asset — Asset Information" caption="(1) Debit Entry, (2) Asset Name, (3) Category, (4) Location, (5) Purchase Cost, (6) Purchase Date, (7) Depreciation Start Date, (8) Description." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the depreciation details
Under **Depreciation Details**, choose the **Depreciation Method**:

| Method | Required field | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Straight Line** | **Useful Life (Years)** | Spreads the depreciation evenly over the useful life of the asset. Each period has the same depreciation expense. |
| **Declining Balance** | **Depreciation Rate (%)** | Applies the rate to the remaining book value each period, so depreciation is larger at first and decreases over time. |

Depreciation begins in the month of acquisition and is calculated
automatically based on the purchase date (**Month-of-Purchase Policy**).

Choose the **Depreciation Account** (the expense account) and the
**Accumulated Depreciation Account**.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/assets-new-2.jpg" alt="New Asset — Depreciation Details" caption="(1) Depreciation Method, (2) Useful Life (Years), (3) Month-of-Purchase Policy, (4) Depreciation Account, (5) Accumulated Depreciation Account." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Preview the depreciation (optional)
Select **Show Depreciation Preview** to see the schedule before you save. The
**Depreciation Preview** shows the total number of months and, for each month
end, the **Depreciation Amount**, **Accumulated Depreciation**, and **Net Book
Value** — so you can see when the asset will be fully depreciated. Select
**Hide Depreciation Preview** to close it.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/assets-depreciation-preview.jpg" alt="Depreciation Preview" caption="(1) Show or hide the preview, (2) the monthly schedule." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Select **Create Asset**, or **Cancel** to discard it.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Depreciation runs automatically at the **end of each month**. The run covers
  every asset with depreciation due — it is not run per asset or per category.
</Callout>

## Viewing an asset

Select **View** on an asset's row.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/assets-detail.jpg" alt="Asset details" caption="(1) Dispose Asset, (2) Asset Overview, (3) Financial Summary, (4) Depreciation Information." />

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Asset Overview** | Name, asset code, Status, Category, Purchase Date, Depreciation Start Date, Location, Depreciation Account, and Accumulated Depreciation Account. |
| **Financial Summary** | Original Cost, Accumulated Depreciation, and Net Book Value. |
| **Depreciation Information** | Depreciation Method, Useful Life, and Last Depreciation Date. |
| **Asset Value** | A chart of the Net Book Value and Accumulated Depreciation as a share of the original cost. |
| **Depreciation Schedule** | Every depreciation entry with its Date, Depreciation Amount, Accumulated Depreciation, Net Book Value, and Status (**Completed** or **Scheduled**). |
| **GL Impact** | The journal entries posted for the asset. |
| **History** | Changes made to the asset. |

## Disposing of an asset

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Dispose Asset
On the asset's page, select **Dispose Asset**. The dialog warns that the action
cannot be undone: disposing updates the asset's status and creates the journal
entries for the disposal. It shows the asset's **Original Cost**,
**Accumulated Depreciation**, and **Net Book Value**.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/assets-dispose.jpg" alt="Dispose Asset dialog" caption="(1) Warning, (2) Current Asset Information, (3) Disposal Type, (4) Disposal Date." width={760} />
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the disposal details
Choose the **Disposal Type** — **Sale**, **Write-off**, or **Destroy** — and
check the **Disposal Date**. For a sale, enter the **Sale Price**; the **Loss
on Disposal** is shown, based on the net book value. Choose the
**Payment Method** and **Payment Account**, and optionally add **Description /
Notes**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm
Select **Dispose Asset**, or **Cancel** to close the dialog. Disposed assets
are listed under the **Disposed** tab.
</Step>

</Steps>
