---
title: Bank Transfers
description: Record transfers of money between accounts.
---

A **Bank Transfer** records money moved from one account to another. AccountWise
posts the journal entry to the General Ledger automatically.

## The Bank Transfers list

Go to **Banking → Bank Transfers**.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/transfer-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Bank Transfers list" caption="(1) New Transfer, (2) Start Date and End Date filters, (3) Search transfers." />

The list shows the **From Account** (with the transfer number), **To Account**,
**Date**, **Location**, **Description**, and **Amount**. Select a row to open
the transfer.

## Creating a transfer

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
On the Bank Transfers page, select **New Transfer**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose the accounts
Select the account the money leaves in **Transfer From** and the account it
goes to in **Transfer To**. Each account is shown with its type, for example
**(Asset)**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the amount and date
Enter the **Transfer Amount**. The **Date** defaults to today; change it if
needed. Optionally choose a **Location** and enter a **Memo**.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/transfer-new.jpg" alt="New Transfer form" caption="(1) Transfer From, (2) Transfer To, (3) Transfer Amount, (4) Date, (5) Location, (6) Memo." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Attach files and save
Optionally drop files under **Attachments** or click **browse** to add them.
Select **Create Transfer**, or **Cancel** to discard the transfer.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Viewing a transfer

Select a transfer in the list to open its details.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/transfer-detail.jpg" alt="Bank transfer details" caption="(1) Back, (2) Transfer #, Date, Amount, and Location, (3) From Account and To Account, (4) GL Impact." />

Expand **GL Impact** to see the journal entry: the **Transfer From** account is
credited and the **Transfer To** account is debited for the transfer amount.
