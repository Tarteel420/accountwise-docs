---
title: Bank Deposits
description: Deposit received payments, and any additional funds, into a bank account.
---

**Bank Deposits** move payments you have received from the **Undeposited
Funds** account into a bank account. You can also add other funds to the same
deposit.

## The Bank Deposits list

Go to **Banking → Bank Deposits**.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/deposit-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Bank Deposits list" caption="(1) New Deposit, (2) Account filter, (3) Start date and End date, (4) Search." />

The list shows the **Account** (with the deposit number), **Date**,
**Description**, **Location**, and **Amount**. Select a row to open the deposit.

## Creating a deposit

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
On the Bank Deposits page, select **New Deposit**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the deposit details
Choose the **Account** to deposit into. The **Date** defaults to today.
Optionally enter a **Description** and choose a **Location**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the payments
Under **Select Payments to Deposit**, tick each payment to include. Each line
shows the document number and who it was **Received From**, its **Date**,
**Type** (for example, **InvoiceReceipt** or **CreditMemo**), and **Amount**.
Use the checkbox in the header to select all. The **Total Amount** at the top
updates as you select payments.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/deposit-new-1.jpg" alt="New Deposit form" caption="(1) Account, (2) Date, (3) Description, (4) Location, (5) Total Amount, (6) tick the payments to deposit." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add other funds (optional)
Under **Add Funds to this Deposit**, select **Add Fund** to add a line. Choose
the **Account** the funds come from, enter a **Description**, **Location**, and
the **Amount**. Use the bin icon to remove a line.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/deposit-new-2.jpg" alt="Add Funds to this Deposit" caption="(1) Add Fund, (2) Account, (3) Description, (4) Location, (5) Amount, (6) remove the line, (7) Create Deposit." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Select **Create Deposit**, or **Cancel** to discard it.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Viewing a deposit

Select a deposit in the list to open its details.

<Screenshot src="/images/banking/deposit-detail.jpg" alt="Bank deposit details" caption="(1) Account, Date, Total Deposited, Description, and Location, (2) Deposit Summary, (3) Payment Details." />

- **Deposit Summary** — **Payments Received**, **Additional Funds**, and
  **Amount Received in Bank**.
- **Payment Details** — the payments included in the deposit.
- **GL Impact** — the journal entry. The bank account is debited and
  **Undeposited Funds** is credited.
