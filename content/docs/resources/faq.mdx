## Do I need to create journal entries myself?

No. AccountWise creates journal entries **automatically** when you record sales
and purchases. You can create manual entries for adjustments. See [Journal
Entries](/docs/accounting/journal-entries).

## Why can't I see other salespeople's customers?

Customers are assigned to salespeople. Each salesperson only sees their own
assigned customers and related documents. See [User
Roles](/docs/getting-started/user-roles) and [Customer
Assignments](/docs/sales/customers/customer-assignments).

## What's the difference between an Invoice and a Sales Receipt?

An [Invoice](/docs/sales/invoices) creates Accounts Receivable and is paid
later. A [Sales Receipt](/docs/sales/sales-receipts) records the sale and
payment together, with no receivable.

## Why can't I void this invoice?

Only **unpaid** invoices can be voided, and void is configuration based — not
every user has it. See [Voiding Sales
Documents](/docs/sales/voiding-sales-documents).

## Can I edit default accounts?

No. Default system accounts cannot be edited or deleted, but you can add your
own. See [Chart of Accounts](/docs/accounting/chart-of-accounts).

## Why don't I see a page in the sidebar?

The sidebar only shows the modules your role has permission to view, and
customization pages only appear for users with full access to the related
document. Ask your administrator to review your role. See [Roles &
Profiles](/docs/administration/roles-and-profiles).

## How do I find a page quickly?

Press **Ctrl K** (or select the search box in the top bar) and type a name or
document number — for example, a customer name or an invoice number. Select a
result to go straight to it, or press **Esc** to close search. See [Global
Search](/docs/getting-started/global-search).

## How do I open the documentation from AccountWise?

Select **Docs** in the top bar. The documentation opens in a new browser tab.

## Why is my document pending approval?

Your organization requires approval for that document type. The document
doesn't take effect until the approver approves it. See [Approval
Workflow](/docs/administration/approvals).

## Can I save a customer without a zip code?

Yes, for addresses outside the USA — **Zip Code** and **State** are optional for
other countries. See [Creating
Customers](/docs/sales/customers/creating-customers).
