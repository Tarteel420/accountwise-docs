A **Refund Receipt** is created when a customer needs to be refunded.

## The Refund Receipts list

Go to **Sales → Refund Receipts**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/refund-receipt-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Refund Receipts list" caption="(1) New Refund Receipt, (2) All, Manual, and Square tabs, (3) Customers, Start date, End date, and Search filters, (4) the Source of each refund." />

- **Tabs** — **All**, **Manual**, and **Square**, each with a count.
- **Columns** — Customer (with the refund receipt number), Refund Receipt
  Date, Amount, **Source** (**Manual** or **Square**), and Actions (**View**).

## Creating a refund receipt

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
Go to **Sales → Refund Receipts** and select **New Refund Receipt**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the customer and refund details
Choose the customer in **Select Customer** — the **Billing Address** is filled
in. Enter the **Reference Number** and **Email**, and check the **Refund Receipt
Date**. Set the **Payment Method** and the account to **Refund From**.
Optionally choose the related **Invoice** and a **Location**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/refund-receipt-new-1.jpg" alt="New Refund Receipt form" caption="(1) Select Customer, (2) Reference Number, (3) Email, (4) Refund Receipt Date, (5) Payment Method, (6) Refund From, (7) Invoice, (8) Location." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add the refund lines
Under **Details**, select the related **Credit Memo** and enter the
**Description**, **Quantity**, and **Rate** — the **Amount** is calculated.
Select **Add Line Item** to add more lines.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/refund-receipt-new-2.jpg" alt="Refund receipt detail lines" caption="(9) Credit Memo, (10) Description, Quantity, Rate, and Amount, (11) Add Line Item." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Add any **Attachments**, complete the **Terms and Conditions** (required), and
select **Create & Send** to complete the refund.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Refund directly from an invoice

Refunds can also be started directly from the invoice screen with **Refund**
(see [Invoices](/docs/sales/invoices#refunding-from-an-invoice)). In that case,
the system automatically creates **both** the credit memo and the refund
receipt.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/invoice-refund-dialog.jpg" alt="Process Refund dialog on an invoice" caption="The Process Refund dialog on an invoice: (1) Refund Amount, (2) Refund Account, (3) Reason, (4) Process Refund." width={760} />

## Refund reason and notifications

When processing a refund, you can add a **refund reason**. It appears in the
audit trail, and the customer is notified by email with the refund status
attached.

<Callout type="info">
  Refund receipts support the [approval workflow](/docs/administration/approvals). When
  your organization turns on **Requires Approval** for this document in
  **Settings → Approval Policy**, a new or changed document must be approved
  before it takes effect.
</Callout>
