---
title: Voiding Sales Documents
description: Void invoices and sales orders, reversing their journal entries.
---

Voiding cancels a sales document and **reverses all of its related journal
entries**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Void is **configuration based** — not everyone has the option to void.
</Callout>

## What can be voided

- **Invoices** — only **unpaid** invoices can be voided.
- **Sales Orders.**

<Callout type="info">
  When an invoice was converted from a sales order and you void the invoice, the
  originating sales order is voided too.
</Callout>

## Void an invoice

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the See more menu
Go to **Sales → Invoices**. On the invoice's row, open the **See more** menu
(**⋮**) and select **Void**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/invoice-row-actions.jpg" alt="See more menu with Void" caption="The See more menu (2) on an invoice row includes Void." width={740} />
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the reason and confirm
The **Confirm Void** dialog asks you to confirm — this action cannot be undone.
Enter the **Reason** and select **Void Invoice**, or **Cancel** to keep the
invoice.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/invoice-void-dialog.jpg" alt="Confirm Void dialog" caption="(1) Enter the void reason. (2) Select Void Invoice." width={760} />
</Step>

</Steps>

Voided invoices appear under the **Void** tab of the Invoices list.

## Void reason

When voiding, you must add a **void reason**. It is included in the email sent
to the customer.
