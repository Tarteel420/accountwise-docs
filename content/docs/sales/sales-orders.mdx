title: Sales Orders

A **Sales Order** confirms what a customer wants before you bill them. It is
created by selecting a customer and adding items.

<Callout type="info">
  If your account is integrated with **Stock Wise**, the item list comes
  directly from Stock Wise. Otherwise, AccountWise's internal items are used
  (**Search AccountWise Items**).
</Callout>

## The Sales Orders list

Go to **Sales → Sales Orders**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/sales-order-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Sales Orders list" caption="(1) New Sales Order, (2) status tabs, (3) Customers, Start date, End date, and Search filters, (4) the invoice created from the order, (5) View, Edit, and Delete." />

- **Status tabs** — **All**, **Draft**, **Pending**, **Approved**,
  **Cancelled**, **Complete**, and **Void**, each with a count.
- **Filters** — **Customers**, **Start date**, **End date**, and **Search**.
- **Columns** — Customer (with the sales order number), Invoice, Work Order,
  Sale Order Date, Payment Term, Quantity, Amount, Status, and Actions.

Once a sales order is converted, the invoice number appears in the **Invoice**
column as a **hyperlink** that opens that invoice.

## Creating a sales order

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
Go to **Sales → Sales Orders** and select **New Sales Order**. You can also
start one from a customer's record with **Create Document → Create Sales
Order**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the customer
Choose the customer in **Select Customer**. The **Customer Balance**, billing
and shipping addresses, email, and payment term populate automatically. Enter a
**Reference number** and check **Tax Type**, **Payment Term**, **Discount
Type**, and **Sale Order Date**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/sales-order-new.jpg" alt="New Sales Order form" caption="(1) Select Customer, (2) Reference number, (3) Email, (4) Tax Type, (5) Payment Term, (6) Discount Type, (7) Sale Order Date, (8) Create Shipnest Order." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Complete the order details
Optionally choose **Create Shipnest Order**, **Sales Person**, and
**Location**, and add a **Memo** and **Shipping Date**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add items
Search for items and use **Add Item** to add lines. Add any **Additional
Surcharge** if needed. The item lines and totals work the same way as on an
invoice — see [Invoices](/docs/sales/invoices#creating-an-invoice-directly).
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Add any **Attachments** and **Terms and Conditions**, then select **Save as
Draft** or **Create & Send**.
</Step>

</Steps>

When a sales order is created:

- Its status is **Pending**.
- It can be **emailed** to the customer.
- The customer can **approve or cancel** it, or a salesperson can approve it
  manually.

<Callout type="info">
  No journal entry is made at this stage — revenue is not yet recognized.
</Callout>

## Viewing a sales order

Select **View** on a row to open the sales order. It is shown as a **Proforma
Invoice**, with the toolbar icons (Back, Edit, View, Download, Clone, Send) and
these actions:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Packing Slip** | Opens the packing slip for the order. |
| **Convert into Invoice** | Converts the sales order into an invoice. |
| **Approve** | Approves a pending sales order. It is greyed out once the order is approved. |

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/sales-order-detail.jpg" alt="Sales order detail page" caption="(1) Toolbar icons, (2) Packing Slip, (3) Convert into Invoice, (4) Approve." />

The order's status is shown next to its number and as a watermark across the
document — for example, **PENDING**. See [Invoices](/docs/sales/invoices#document-status-and-watermark).

<Callout type="info">
  Sales orders also support the [approval
  workflow](/docs/administration/approvals). When approvals are turned on for
  sales orders, a new or changed order must be approved before it takes effect.
</Callout>

## With Stock Wise integration

If Stock Wise is integrated, a **Work Order** can be created from the sales
order in **Draft** status. Once the sales order is approved, the work order
status becomes **Open**.

## Converting to an invoice

To convert a sales order into an [Invoice](/docs/sales/invoices):

<Steps>

<Step>
### Complete the stock steps (Stock Wise only)
If Stock Wise is integrated, complete and close the Work Order in Stock Wise,
then stock out the inventory.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select Convert into Invoice
Open the sales order and select **Convert into Invoice**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Apply or skip credit memos
The **Credit Memos** dialog lists the available credit memos with their
**Credit Date**, **Original Amount**, and **Opening Balance**. To apply credit,
tick a credit memo and enter the **Amount** to use, then select **Apply Credit
Memo**. Otherwise select **Skip Credit Memo**. Select **Close** to cancel.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/sales-order-convert-credit-memo.jpg" alt="Credit Memos dialog when converting a sales order" caption="(1) Tick a credit memo, (2) enter the amount to apply, (3) Skip Credit Memo, (4) Apply Credit Memo." />
</Step>

</Steps>

Once converted, the invoice number appears on the sales order listing page as a
**hyperlink** that opens that invoice.
