Customers are the starting point of the sales cycle. All sales documents pull
their information from the customer record.

Customers are managed from **Customers Management** in the sidebar, which
contains **Customers**, **Activities**, and **Customer Assignments**.

## The Customers list

Go to **Customers Management → Customers**. The list shows each customer's
**Shipping Address**, **Billing Address**, **Tags**, **Sales Rep**,
**Hierarchy**, **Business Unit**, **Corporate**, and **Date Created**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/customers/customer-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Customers list with toolbar controls highlighted" caption="(1) New customer, (2) Search, (3) Columns, (4) Advance Filters, (5) Filters, (6) View settings." />

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **New customer** | Opens the form to create a customer. See [Creating Customers](/docs/sales/customers/creating-customers). |
| **Search customers...** | Searches the list. The search term appears as a chip above the list; select **Clear** to remove it. |
| **Columns** | Choose which columns are shown. |
| **Advance Filters** | Build a filter from a **Column**, an **Operator** (for example, **Equals**), and a **Value**. |
| **Filters** | Opens the **Filters** panel to filter by **Sales person** and **Customer Tags**. |
| **View settings** (gear icon) | Choose whether **Active**, **Inactive**, and **Archived** customers are shown. |

## Quick view

Select a customer's name in the list to open a quick view. It shows the
customer's status, shipping and billing addresses, contacts, and profile details
such as payment terms, payment method, and branches. Select **View Details** to
open the full customer record.
