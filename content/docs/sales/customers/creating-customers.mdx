---
title: Creating Customers
description: Add a new customer with its primary information, addresses, profile, and contacts.
---

New customers are created from the **Create a new commercial customer** form.

## Create a customer

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
Go to **Customers Management → Customers** and select **New customer** in the
top-right corner.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/customers/customer-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Customers list with New customer highlighted" caption="Select New customer (1) to open the form." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter primary information
Under **Primary information**, enter the **Customer name**. **Type** is set to
**Commercial**, and **Status** defaults to **Customer**. You can also enter:

- **Parent company**
- **Lead source**
- **Competitor**
- **Doing Business As (DBA)**
- **Business unit**
- **Sales rep**
- **EPA #**
- **Website**

Turn on any of the toggles that apply: **Send statement**, **Cooperate
customer**, **Opportunity for old customer**, **Send COR with invoice**, **R2
Certified**, **Bill root parent**, and **Create as vendor**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/customers/new-customer-primary.jpg" alt="Primary information section of the new customer form" caption="(1) Customer name, (2) Type, (3) Status, (4) Business unit, (5) customer toggles, (6) Doing Business As (DBA)." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the addresses
Under **Addresses**, complete the **Shipping** and **Billing** addresses —
**Address Line 1**, **Address Line 2**, **City**, **State**, **Zip Code**, and
**Country**. If both addresses are the same, fill in one and select **Copy
Billing address** (on the shipping side) or **Copy Shipping address** (on the
billing side).

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/customers/new-customer-addresses.jpg" alt="Addresses section with copy buttons" caption="(1) Shipping address fields, (2) Copy Billing address, (3) Copy Shipping address." />

<Callout type="info">
  **Zip Code** and **State** are optional for countries other than the **USA**.
  You can save a customer with an address outside the USA that has no zip code
  or state, and you can create sales documents for that customer as normal.
  Your administrator controls this in the [Customer Fields
  Map](/docs/administration/customer-fields-map).
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter profile information
Under **Profile information**, set the **Payment Method** and **Payment Term**.
Turn on **Requires P.O #** if the customer needs a purchase order number, then
enter the **P.O #** and **PO Expiry**. You can also enter:

- **Feet** and **Inches** (Clearance Height)
- **Starting hours** and **Ending hours**
- **Branches**
- **Additional surcharges**
- **Guaranteed destruction**
- **Tags**

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/customers/new-customer-profile.jpg" alt="Profile information and Contacts sections" caption="(1) Requires P.O #, (2) Payment Method and Payment Term, (3) Additional surcharges, Guaranteed destruction, and Tags, (4) Add Contact." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add contacts
Under **Contacts**, select **Add Contact**. In **Add Contact details**, enter the
**Contact type**, **First name**, **Email**, and **Phone #**. **Last name**,
**Title**, **Mobile #**, and **Extension** are optional. Select **Add**. Repeat
for each contact.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/customers/add-contact.jpg" alt="Add Contact details dialog" caption="The Add Contact details dialog. Select Add (1) to add the contact to the customer." width={760} />
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Select **Save** at the bottom of the form to create the customer, or **Cancel**
to discard it.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/customers/new-customer-save.jpg" alt="Save and Cancel buttons at the bottom of the form" caption="Select Save (5) to create the customer." />
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Which fields are required on this form is set by your administrator in the
  [Customer Fields Map](/docs/administration/customer-fields-map). Fields marked
  with an asterisk (*) must be filled in before you can save.
</Callout>

## Customer options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Parent company | Links the customer to a parent customer. The relationship appears in the **Hierarchy** column and on the parent's **Sub Customers** tab. |
| Bill root parent | Enables bill-to-parent behavior. |
| Create as vendor | Treats the customer as a vendor too. |
| Send statement | Emails the customer a report of outstanding invoices and credit memos every two weeks (configuration based). |
| R2 Certified | Flags whether the customer is R2 certified; visible in customer details. |

## Assigning a salesperson

Select the **Sales rep** on the form, or assign the customer later from
[Customer Assignments](/docs/sales/customers/customer-assignments). Once
assigned, that salesperson can only see their own assigned customers and related
documents. See [User Roles](/docs/getting-started/user-roles).
