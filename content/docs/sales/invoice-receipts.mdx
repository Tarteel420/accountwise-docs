---
title: Invoice Receipts
description: Record a customer's payment against one or more invoices.
---

An **Invoice Receipt** records payment received from a customer.

## Two ways to record

- Select **Mark as Paid** on the invoice. You can optionally attach a file. See
  [Invoices](/docs/sales/invoices#recording-payment).
- Create a receipt from **Sales → Receipts → Invoice Receipts**, as described
  below. This lets you apply one payment to several invoices.

## The Invoice Receipts list

Go to **Sales → Receipts → Invoice Receipts**. The list shows the **Customer
Name** (with the receipt number), **Invoice Receipt Date**, and **Amount**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/invoice-receipt-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Invoice Receipts list" caption="(1) New Invoice Receipt, (2) Customers filter, (3) Search, (4) View and the See more menu." />

Use **View** (eye icon) to open a receipt. The **See more** menu (**⋮**) offers
**Download**.

## Recording a payment

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Receive Payment form
On the Invoice Receipts page, select **New Invoice Receipt**. The **Receive
Payment** form opens.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the customer and payment details
Choose the **Customer**. The amount being received and the **Customer balance**
appear on the right. Then choose the **Account** where payment is received,
and check the **Payment Date** and **Payment Method** (the customer's payment
method is filled in). Optionally select a **Location**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/invoice-receipt-new-1.jpg" alt="Receive Payment form" caption="(1) Customer, (2) amount and customer balance, (3) Account, (4) Payment Date, (5) Payment Method, (6) Location, (7) Amount Received, (8) Use Credit Memos." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the amount and apply it to invoices
Enter the **Amount Received**. Under **Invoice Items**, the customer's open
invoices are listed with their **Due Date**, **Original Amount**, and **Open
Balance**. Tick the invoices being paid and enter the **Payment** for each —
full or partial payment is supported. Use **Find Invoice No.** to find a
specific invoice.

Tick **Use Credit Memos** to apply an existing credit memo to the payment.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/invoice-receipt-new-2.jpg" alt="Invoice Items on the Receive Payment form" caption="(9) Find Invoice No., (10) tick the invoices being paid, (11) enter the payment for each invoice." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add attachments and save
Optionally attach files under **Attachments**. Select **Create**, or **Create &
Send** to email the receipt to the customer. Select **Cancel** to discard it.
</Step>

</Steps>

If the payment is partial, the invoice status becomes **Partial** and Accounts
Receivable updates accordingly.

<Callout type="info">
  There is a configuration where, when an invoice receipt is sent to a customer,
  the paid invoices are attached to it as well.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Invoice receipts support the [approval workflow](/docs/administration/approvals). When
  your organization turns on **Requires Approval** for this document in
  **Settings → Approval Policy**, a new or changed document must be approved
  before it takes effect.
</Callout>
