The Sales module covers everything on the money-in side of the business: your
**customers** and the **sales documents** used to bill them and collect payment.

Customers are managed from **Customers Management** in the sidebar. Sales
documents are under **Sales**: **Invoices**, **Receipts** (**Invoice Receipts**
and **Sales Receipts**), **Sales Orders**, **Credit Memos**, **Refund
Receipts**, and **Additional Surcharge**.

## Common behaviors

Across all sales documents:

- You can **clone** documents to save time.
- Listing pages can be **filtered by customer**, and most can also be filtered
  by date and searched.
- A **Paid Amount** field is shown on the Invoices list.
- The **rate** field accepts up to **4 decimal places**.
- Every document shows **History** and **GL Impact** at the bottom.
