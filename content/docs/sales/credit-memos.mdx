A **Credit Memo** is created in situations such as:

- Customer advance payments
- Sales returns
- Adjustments for future orders
- Refunds

## The Credit Memos list

Go to **Sales → Credit Memos**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/credit-memo-list-annotated.jpg" alt="Credit Memos list" caption="(1) New Credit Memo, (2) status tabs, (3) Customers, Start date, End date, and Search filters, (4) View, Edit, Delete, and See more." />

- **Status tabs** — **All**, **Draft**, **Created**, **Partial**, and
  **Applied**, each with a count.
- **Columns** — Customer (with the credit memo number), Credit Memo Date,
  Amount, Applied Amount, Status, and Actions.
- Row actions — **View**, **Edit**, **Delete**, and **See more → Clone**.

## Creating a credit memo

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
Go to **Sales → Credit Memos** and select **New Credit Memo**. You can also
start one from a customer's record with **Create Document → Create Credit
Memo**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the customer and enter the header details
Choose the customer in **Select Customer** — the **Billing Address** is filled
in from the customer record. Enter a **Reference number** and **Email**, and
check the **Credit Memo Date**. Optionally link a **Sale Order Number** (this is
not mandatory) and choose a **Location**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/credit-memo-new-1.jpg" alt="New Credit Memo form" caption="(1) Select Customer, (2) Reference number, (3) Email, (4) Sale Order Number, (5) Credit Memo Date, (6) Location." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add details and account
Under **Details**, enter a **Description**, select the appropriate
**Account**, and enter the **Quantity** and **Rate** — the **Amount** is
calculated. Select **Add Item** to add more lines.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/credit-memo-new-2.jpg" alt="Credit memo detail lines" caption="(7) Description, (8) Account, (9) Quantity, Rate, and Amount, (10) Add Item." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Add any **Attachments**, complete the **Terms and Conditions** (required), and
select **Save as Draft** or **Create & Send**. The credit memo is then
available to apply later.
</Step>

</Steps>

You may select a related [Sales Order](/docs/sales/sales-orders), but it is
**not mandatory**.

Depending on the scenario, you select the appropriate account:

| Scenario | Account type |
| --- | --- |
| Advance payment | Asset account |
| Sales return | Sales account |

<Callout type="info">
  Credit memos support the [approval workflow](/docs/administration/approvals).
  When approvals are turned on for credit memos, a new or changed credit memo
  must be approved before it takes effect.
</Callout>

## Applying a credit memo

Credit memos can later be applied during:

- [Invoice](/docs/sales/invoices) creation
- [Invoice Receipt](/docs/sales/invoice-receipts) creation (tick **Use Credit Memos**)
- Converting a [Sales Order](/docs/sales/sales-orders#converting-to-an-invoice) into an invoice

<Callout type="info">
  Credit memos also affect customer balances in reports. See [Customer
  Reports](/docs/reports/customer-reports).
</Callout>
