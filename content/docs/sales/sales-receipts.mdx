title: Sales Receipts
description: Record an immediate sale and payment in a single step.
---

A **Sales Receipt** is used when a sale and its payment happen at the same time
— typically in retail scenarios.

## How it differs from an invoice

Unlike an [Invoice](/docs/sales/invoices), a sales receipt does **not** create
Accounts Receivable. It records both revenue and payment in one step.

## The Sale Receipts list

Go to **Sales → Receipts → Sales Receipts**. The list shows the **Customer
Name** (with the receipt number), **Sale Receipt Date**, and **Amount**.

## Creating a sales receipt

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form
On the Sale Receipts page, select **New Sale Receipt**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the customer
Choose the customer in **Select Customer**. The **Billing Address** and
**Email** are filled in from the customer record; use the pencil icon to change
the billing address.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the receipt and payment details
Enter a **Reference number** and check the **Tax Type**, **Discount Type**, and
**Sale Receipt Date**. Choose the **Payment Account** the money is received
into and the **Payment Method** (for example, **Credit Card**). Optionally
choose a **Sales Person** and **Location**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sales/sales-receipt-new-1.jpg" alt="New Sale Receipt form" caption="(1) Select Customer, (2) Billing Address, (3) Reference number, (4) Email, (5) Tax Type, (6) Discount Type, (7) Sale Receipt Date, (8) Payment Account, (9) Payment Method." />
</Step>

<Step>
### Add items
Under **Details**, use **Search AccountWise Items** or **Add Item** to add the
items sold, and **Add Additional Surcharge** if needed. Enter any **tax** and
**discount**. The item lines and totals work the same way as on an invoice —
see [Invoices](/docs/sales/invoices#creating-an-invoice-directly).
</Step>

<Step>
### Save
Add any **Attachments**, complete the **Terms and Conditions** (required), and
select **Create**, or **Create & Send** to email the receipt to the customer.
Both the revenue and payment are recorded in a single step.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Sales receipts support the [approval workflow](/docs/administration/approvals). When
  your organization turns on **Requires Approval** for this document in
  **Settings → Approval Policy**, a new or changed document must be approved
  before it takes effect.
</Callout>
